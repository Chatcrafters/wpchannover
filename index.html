TennisBase Hannover

One of Germany's premier tennis and racket sport facilities — the official Federal Training Base (Bundesstützpunkt) of the German Tennis Federation. Professional indoor and outdoor courts in the heart of northern Germany.

Professional Facility

Federal Training Base with multiple indoor courts, changing rooms, and full match infrastructure. Used by German national tennis programs — now hosting WPC Series Europe.

Easy to Reach

Hannover is one of Germany's best-connected cities. Direct rail links from Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Cologne and Munich. Hannover Airport with European connections.

Accommodation

Wide range of hotels in Hannover city centre. Multiple options 5–20 minutes from the venue by public transport or taxi.

International Field

WPC Series Europe tournaments consistently attract 20–30 nations with fewer than 50% of players from the host country. Hannover will be no different.
